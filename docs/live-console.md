# Detached live console

Open the stage from the Laser view. The original tab becomes a full-screen operator desk; audio remains in that tab. Move the show tab to the output screen. Return stage closes that output and restores the local preview.

Live shows laser, lighting, and video banks together on desktop. On narrow screens, use Lasers, Lighting, Visuals, or Stage to open each bank. Patterns and videos have page controls instead of scroll lists. Transport, blackout, momentary flash, and output faders remain visible. Native buttons, labels, color inputs, and sliders support keyboard use; flash releases on key-up, pointer cancellation, and focus loss.

Laser bank targeting applies patterns to top, floor, or both. Lighting has independent speed, movement range, pattern, and palette; the ceiling trim can have its own color. Light patterns drive ceiling, center, and main moving heads. Off extinguishes the lighting layer. Run song show restores automatic patterns and palettes and clears blackout; output levels stay under operator control.

Stage contains stage design, camera, screen layout, layer selection, visual timing, and scanner spread. VFX Fit preserves the whole clip; Fill crops to the screen aspect ratio.

Validation: type checking and targeted ESLint; browser fit and accessible-name checks at 1280 x 720; live pattern/color/speed selection with detached rendering and continuing audio. Screenshots: /tmp/vault-compact-live-desk.png and /tmp/vault-stage-rig-updated.png.
